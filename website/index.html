FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Luna is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Why isn’t it on Google Play yet?

We’re in an early release. You can install it directly from this page today. A Google Play and iPhone release is planned, and your data will carry over when you update.

Can I use it for birth control?

No. Predictions are estimates for awareness only. Luna is not a contraceptive and not a medical device. Talk to a doctor about contraception or any health concern.

What happens if I lose my phone or forget my PIN?

Your data exists only on your phone, so we can’t recover it. That’s the trade-off for real privacy. If you forget your PIN and haven’t turned on fingerprint or face unlock, you can erase the app’s data and start again.

Does Luna work without internet?

Yes, completely. The app never needs a connection.

Legal

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · applies to the Luna app and this website.

In short: Luna does not collect, store, share or sell any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays encrypted on your own device.

Who we are

Luna is made by COdesign (Dhaka, Bangladesh). You can contact us at the email address shown below.

Information the app stores on your device

When you use the app, the following is saved only on your phone:

  • The name you choose to enter (optional)
  • Your typical cycle and period length
  • The days you log, including period days, flow, symptoms, moods, energy and notes
  • Your settings for reminders and app lock
  • If you set a PIN, a one-way scrambled version of it (a salted hash), never the PIN itself

This information is stored in an encrypted database (AES-256). The encryption key is generated on your device and kept in your phone’s secure keystore. We never receive it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. It does not send your health information over the internet, and we have no server that stores it. Because we never have your data, we cannot sell it, share it, or hand it to anyone, including advertisers, data brokers or authorities.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone. No notification service or push server is used. By default, reminders use discreet wording so nothing about your cycle appears on your lock screen. You can turn notifications off at any time.

Device permissions

  • Notifications (optional): only to show the reminders you turn on.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock (optional): handled entirely by your phone’s operating system. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded, never your biometric data.

Backups

To keep your data private, the app opts out of automatic cloud backups. This means uninstalling the app or losing your phone permanently deletes your data.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time in Settings → Delete all data. This removes every log, setting, your PIN and all scheduled reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and all fonts and images are served from this site. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and operations.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Since no data is collected at all, no information about children is collected either.

Health disclaimer

Predictions are estimates based on the information you enter. Luna is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and must not be used as contraception.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change ever affected how data is handled, we would explain it in the app before it took effect.
